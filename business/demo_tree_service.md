A fictional tree service used for demos and testing. Swap this file for your own business.

- Owner: Sam (signs emails as "Sam, Cedar & Sky Tree Care")
- Phone: 705-555-0100 (answered 7 am to 7 pm, 7 days; emergencies any time)
- Service area: Collingwood, the Blue Mountains, Thornbury, Wasaga Beach, Stayner, Creemore and Clearview, anywhere within about 45 minutes of Collingwood
- Credentials: ISA certified arborist on staff, fully insured, WSIB covered
- In business since 2015

## Services and typical pricing
- Free on-site estimates, usually within 2 business days
- Tree removal: most jobs $800 to $3,500 depending on size, access and how close it is to buildings
- Emergency and storm-damage response: 24/7, $250 call-out plus hourly, we can usually be on site the same day
- Pruning and crown reduction: from $350 per tree
- Stump grinding: from $150 per stump
- Hedge trimming and seasonal maintenance: quoted per property, discounts for multi-property and repeat customers
- Firewood and wood chips left on site free of charge if wanted

## How we work
- Photos and the address help us give a faster and more accurate estimate
- Every job is quoted in writing before work starts, no surprise charges
- Payment by e-transfer, card or cheque, invoice sent the day the job is finished
- Some towns require a permit to remove larger trees; when a permit is needed we prepare the paperwork as part of the job

## Tone for replies
Friendly, plain, no jargon. Sound like a local business owner, not a call centre. Never quote a firm price for a removal without seeing the tree; give the typical range and offer a free estimate.
